The small planetary nebula Abell 71 (PK 085+04.1), also catalogued as Sh 2-116, is visible in the lower-right part of the image; it lies in the foreground and is not physically associated with Sh 2-115. The image was processed in narrowband using an SHO combination and the Hubble palette.

PK 085+04.1, also known as Abell 71 or Sh 2-116, lies very close by in the same field of view in Cygnus, at an estimated distance of between 2,400 and 3,100 light-years from Earth. For many years it was classified as a planetary nebula with an almost circular, nearly spherical morphology. More recent astrophysical research, however, has shown that this appearance conceals a much more complex ellipsoidal structure that interacts closely with the Sh 2-115 hydrogen-emission region. Its proximity to and physical overlap with the surrounding interstellar medium continue to fuel debate among astronomers over whether it is purely an isolated planetary nebula or a star-forming region with unusual characteristics.
