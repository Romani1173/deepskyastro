The nebula displays a complex structure formed by two large shells on either side of 68 Cygni, with emission arcs, delicate filaments, cavities and dark clouds silhouetted against Cygnus’s dense star field. In the image, the hydrogen emissions, represented in golden tones, contrast with the bluish oxygen emissions surrounding 68 Cygni and highlight the effects of its radiation and stellar winds on the interstellar gas.

The image was processed in narrowband using an HOO combination and treated with PixInsight’s NarrowbandNormalization script to balance and enhance the different emissions.

At the centre of the field is the tiny planetary nebula PK 87-3.1, also catalogued as PN G087.4−03.8 and Wein 2-245. It appears to be superimposed on the vast expanse of Sh2-119: a small envelope of gas expelled by a star at the end of its life, projected against an extensive region of ionised hydrogen. This contrast between two nebulae of very different nature and scale makes the field particularly interesting.
